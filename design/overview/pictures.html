How the pictures were made

  • From the live sites, on Sunday November 1st 2026 at about 2.30pm, in Chromium.
  • Sizes: computer at 1440px wide; phone at 390px wide with touch. Very tall pages are cut into halves (one per page) or into strips shown side by side, read left to right.
  • Content: pictures marked sample use content typed in for the picture (a running order, a week, a flowchart). Clockmaker's clock is its built-in Example. Pictures marked real show the sites' real figures at the time: Hindcaster's final update for Saturday October 31st, Lightreader's times for Caversham (RG4), and Refueller's prices from the 10.15am collection.
  • Checks: no page errors and no sideways scrolling on any picture, and the brand fonts loaded every time.
  • Light mode throughout, apart from the dark-mode page near the end.
