How the pictures were made

  • The public sites, from the live sites on Wednesday November 11th 2026 at about 11.30am, in Chromium.
  • Sizes: computer at 1440px wide; phone at 390px wide with touch. Very tall pages are cut into halves (one per page) or into strips shown side by side, read left to right.
  • Content: pictures marked sample use content typed in for the picture (a running order, a week, a flowchart). Clockmaker's clock is its built-in Example. Pictures marked real show the sites' real figures at the time: Hindcaster's final update for Tuesday November 10th, Lightreader's and Nightreader's times for Caversham (RG4), Refueller's prices from the 10.15am collection, Riverwatcher's levels from the 11.10am update, and Roadteller's collision figures for 2021 to 2025.
  • Tenniserver and Motoracer (private, behind a sign-in): built from their repos on Thursday November 12th 2026, with the real data saved there (Tenniserver's 10am update; Motoracer's of 5.50pm on November 11th).
  • Checks: no page errors and no sideways scrolling on any picture, and the brand fonts loaded every time.
  • Light mode throughout, apart from the dark-mode page and the private sites' dark looks.
